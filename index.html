FAQ

Answers before you commit.

What are home inspection repairs?

They're the fixes an inspector recommends to bring a property up to safety and quality standards: anything from a missing handrail or a grading issue to structural and drainage problems. In a real estate transaction, some become contractually required before closing. We complete them correctly and on time so the deal keeps moving.

Can you quote directly from my inspection report?

Yes, that's exactly how we work. Send the PDF (or photos of the pages) and we return an itemized, line-by-line quote, typically within 24 hours. You can have us fix everything or only the items your contract requires.

How fast can repairs be completed before closing?

We offer prompt scheduling built around closing and inspection deadlines. Tell us your date and we work backwards from it. Most repair lists can be completed well inside a typical closing window. On a tight timeline, call us at (856) 516-1347.

What types of issues do you repair?

Structural and foundation fixes, drainage and grading corrections, concrete and hardscape, roofing and flashing, siding and rot, deck and railing safety items, interior punch-list work and insurance claim damage. If it's on a report, we've likely fixed it.

Do you work directly with realtors or homeowners?

Both, all the time. We collaborate directly with homeowners, realtors and inspectors, keep everyone updated, and deliver the documentation the transaction needs: itemized invoices, photos and completion summaries ready for the re-inspection or the closing file.

Will the repairs pass re-inspection?

That's the standard we work to: inspection compliance, guaranteed. Repairs are completed to meet the inspection's requirements, and we hand you a paper trail of photos and receipts, so verification is straightforward.

How much do inspection repairs cost?

It depends entirely on what's flagged, which is why we price line by line from your actual report instead of quoting vague lump sums. You see the cost of every item individually, fix what the contract requires, and skip what it doesn't. The estimate is free and carries no obligation.

Can you repair only some items on the report?

Absolutely. Because the quote is itemized, you can hand us just the contractually required repairs, just the safety items, or the whole list. Your call, with pricing transparent either way.

Can you handle lender-required (FHA / VA) repairs before closing?

When a lender's appraiser flags items like peeling paint, missing handrails or broken outlets, the loan can't close until they're fixed and re-verified. We prioritize lender-required repairs, complete them to the flagged requirements, and provide the documentation needed for re-verification.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, 0% financing options are available on qualifying projects. If an unexpected repair list landed in the middle of your move, ask about payment options when we prepare your estimate.

What areas do you serve?

South Jersey (Gloucester, Camden and Burlington Counties) and the surrounding tri-state area, from our base in Woodbury Heights, NJ. Not sure about your town? Call and ask.
