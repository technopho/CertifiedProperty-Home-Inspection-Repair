Questions & answers

Home inspection repairs, explained

What are home inspection repairs?

They're the fixes an inspector recommends to bring a property up to safety and quality standards — anything from a missing handrail or GFCI outlet to grading, drainage or structural issues. In a real estate transaction, some become contractually required before closing. We complete them correctly and on time so the deal keeps moving.

Can you quote directly from my inspection report?

Yes — that's exactly how we work. Send the PDF (or photos of the pages) and we'll return an itemized, line-by-line quote, typically within 24 hours. You can have us fix everything or only the items your contract requires.

How fast can repairs be completed before closing?

We offer prompt scheduling built around closing and inspection deadlines. Tell us your date and we'll work backwards from it — most repair lists can be completed well inside a typical closing window. If the timeline is tight, call us today at (856) 516-1347.

What types of issues do you repair?

Structural and foundation fixes, drainage and grading corrections, roofing and exterior repairs, electrical safety items (GFCI outlets, double-tapped breakers), plumbing leaks and water heaters, concrete cracks, wood rot, handrails, and the smoke & CO detector placement NJ's fire certificate requires for resale. If it's on a report, we've likely fixed it.

Do you work with realtors and buyers' agents?

All the time. We collaborate directly with homeowners, realtors and inspectors, keep everyone updated, and deliver the documentation the transaction needs — itemized invoices, photos and completion summaries ready for the re-inspection or the closing file.

Who pays for inspection repairs — the buyer or the seller?

It's negotiable. Sellers often complete agreed repairs before closing; sometimes buyers accept a credit and handle repairs themselves after settlement. We work both sides — completing sellers' repair addendum items on deadline, or helping buyers turn a repair credit into properly finished work.

Will the repairs pass the re-inspection?

That's the standard we work to: inspection compliance, guaranteed. Repairs are completed to meet the inspection requirements, and we hand you the paper trail — photos and receipts — so verification is straightforward.

Can you handle lender-required (FHA / VA) repairs before closing?

When a lender's appraiser flags items like peeling paint, missing handrails or broken outlets, the loan can't close until they're fixed and re-verified. We prioritize lender-required repairs, complete them to the flagged requirements, and provide the documentation needed for re-verification.

Do you fix items from a township CO / resale inspection?

Yes. Many NJ towns require a certificate of occupancy or resale inspection before a home changes hands — and the fire certificate requires properly placed smoke & CO detectors. We repair the items the township flags and get the property ready for its re-inspection.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — 0% financing options are available on qualifying projects. If an unexpected repair list landed in the middle of your move, ask about payment options when we prepare your estimate.
